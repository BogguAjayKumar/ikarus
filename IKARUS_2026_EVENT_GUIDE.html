1. Executive Summary & Event Overview

IKARUS 2026 is the premier cybersecurity fest hosted at KG Reddy College of Engineering & Technology (KGRCET) as part of the IGNUS College Fest. Designed specifically for B.Tech engineering students, IKARUS 2026 evaluates real-world OSINT investigation, steganography, digital forensics, and cryptographic password cracking across 3 customized academic year tracks.

Metric / Field Detail Specification
Host Venue CSE Department Computer Labs (Lab 1 - Lab 4), KGRCET Campus
Target Participants B.Tech 1st Year, 2nd Year, and 3rd Year Students
Competition Platform Live Workstation Portal (http://localhost:5173/)
Evaluation Basis Accuracy, Technical Reasoning, & Fastest Completion Time

3. Detailed Competition Track Specifications

1st Year Track The Digital Footprint Hunt

Format: Individual or Pairs (30–45 Mins)

Round 1 (Clue Tracing): Inspect target profile evidence for Aarav Mehta (aarav.m_21) including user_profile.txt, email_conversation.eml, sms_logs.txt, website.png, social_media.txt, and transactions.csv.

Round 2 (Spot the Scam): Analyze 10 realistic emails/SMS messages, classify them as Phishing vs Safe, and provide technical reasoning.

2nd Year Track Steg-Ops: Hidden in Plain Sight

Format: Teams of 2 (45 Mins)

Round 1 (Image Forensics): Extract hidden data from sample_evidence.png using steghide, EXIF metadata tags, or strings commands.

Round 2 (Cipher Break): Decode multi-stage Base64, ROT13, and Caesar ciphers to submit the secret flag:

3rd Year Track Operation: Decrypt & UnLock

Format: Teams of 2 (45–60 Mins)

Round 1 (Hash Reverse Lookup): Reverse target SHA-256 hash string (8d969eef6ecad...) to plaintext password (kgrcet2026) via rainbow tables / CrackStation.

Round 2 (Archive Extraction): Unlock encrypted protected_archive.zip to retrieve flag.txt:
